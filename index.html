How to play

  • Notes fall on the beat of the hamster's real wheel. Tap the matching lane (or press D F J K) as a note crosses the gold line.
  • The tempo follows his live RPM, clamped to a playable 48–176 BPM. If he slows down, so does the track.
  • If Squeektoshi stops for a nap mid-set, the set ends and you get a summary with the real distance he covered while you played.
  • Asleep right now? Play a clearly-labelled practice set at a fixed tempo — no fake hamster data, ever.

Data: the public Squeekchain API (api.squeekchain.com/v1/status), polled every 3 seconds. Squeekchain runs on Ethereum Sepolia testnet. No wallet, no tokens, no transactions — points only, stored in your browser.
